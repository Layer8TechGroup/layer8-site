Assessment Tools

Know Where You Stand.

Structured assessments that surface what matters — whether you're improving operations, protecting valuation, or evaluating a transaction.

🔧

CompanyTuneUp

Business health assessment across 8 operational domains — finance, technology, operations, HR, marketing, legal, risk, and owner dependence. Current state, gap analysis, and prioritized improvement roadmap.

Best for: established SMBs that want a clear picture of operational health before investing in growth or preparing for a future exit.

companytuneup.com ↗
📊

SellerDiligence

Exit readiness assessment for business owners considering a sale. Identifies issues that reduce valuation or complicate a transaction — before a buyer finds them. Includes pre-sale remediation roadmap.

Best for: owners 12–36 months from a potential exit who want to protect their valuation and close faster.

sellerdiligence.com ↗
⚖️

TransactionDiligence

Technology and operational due diligence for business brokers, advisors, and acquirers. Standardized assessment across sell-side readiness and buy-side risk — scored and documented for transaction use.

Best for: brokers and advisors who need consistent, credible technology assessment built into their deal process.
