The Problem

When Infrastructure Fails, Operations Stop

→
Warehouse scanners drop off Wi-Fi during peak shifts
→
Cabling runs are unlabeled, untested, or undocumented
→
Conference rooms require IT every time someone presents
→
Cameras have blind spots and no defined retention policy
→
Access credentials outlive employees and contractors
→
No documentation exists for what was installed or when
Our Role

Layer8 helps organizations assess their physical technology infrastructure, identify what needs to change, and develop a clear plan for getting there. We work alongside your team and licensed installation partners — we own the strategy, the specifications, and the outcomes.

Services

Six Advisory Disciplines. One Plan.

🔍

Infrastructure Assessments

Evaluate existing environments, connectivity challenges, documentation gaps, and operational requirements. Deliverable: written assessment with findings and prioritized recommendations.

📶

Network Architecture & Wireless Planning

Design network topology, segmentation strategy, and wireless coverage plans. Specify equipment, AP placement, and VLAN architecture for new builds and retrofits.

🔌

Structured Cabling & Fiber Planning

Assess cabling infrastructure, document requirements, specify standards-compliant designs, and evaluate proposals from appropriately licensed installation contractors.

🖥️

AV & Collaboration Design

Design conference room AV systems, specify platform-compatible equipment, and develop integration requirements for Zoom Rooms, Teams Rooms, and digital signage deployments.

🛡️

Security Technology Advisory

Technology selection, system requirements, integration planning, and vendor evaluation for IP surveillance, access control, and physical security management platforms.

📋

Implementation Planning & Vendor Coordination

Develop project specifications, evaluate licensed contractor proposals, coordinate implementation sequencing, and validate deliverables against design intent.
