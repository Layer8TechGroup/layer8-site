The Problem

Cyber Insurance Has Changed.
Most SMBs Haven't Caught Up.

Underwriters used to ask a handful of yes/no questions and issue a policy. That's over. Today's applications ask for evidence of specific controls — MFA on email and remote access, endpoint detection and response, privileged access management, incident response plans, and backup architecture that survives ransomware.

Organizations that can't answer those questions accurately — or that answer incorrectly and then file a claim — face coverage denial, policy rescission, or outright rejection at renewal. Most SMBs in the 50–300 employee range are operating blind: they don't know what their insurer is actually evaluating, and they don't know where the gaps are until it's too late.

⚠

Coverage Denied After a Claim

Insurer finds that the controls attested to on the application weren't actually in place. Policy is voided retroactively.

⚠

Rejected at Renewal

Underwriter scores your environment during renewal and declines to renew — or doubles your premium — based on control gaps you didn't know existed.

⚠

Inaccurate Application

You answer "yes" to controls you believe are in place. They're not configured correctly, or not consistently applied. That's a material misrepresentation.

The Assessment

What the CIPU Assessment Covers.

A structured evaluation of the security controls cyber underwriters actually assess — scored, documented, and delivered with a remediation roadmap.

🔐

Identity & Access Controls

MFA enforcement across email, remote access, and privileged accounts. Privileged access management, admin account segmentation, and service account hygiene.

  • MFA coverage and enforcement scope
  • Privileged access management (PAM)
  • Admin account inventory and controls
  • Remote access architecture (VPN, zero-trust)
🖥️

Endpoint & Network Security

Endpoint detection and response (EDR) deployment and coverage, network segmentation, patch management cadence, and vulnerability management posture.

  • EDR deployment and coverage percentage
  • Patch management and cadence
  • Network segmentation and firewall posture
  • Vulnerability scanning and remediation
💾

Backup & Recovery Architecture

Backup coverage, immutability, and air-gap status. Recovery time and recovery point objectives. Whether backups survive a ransomware event that compromises the primary environment.

  • Backup coverage and frequency
  • Immutable and offsite backup verification
  • RTO / RPO against business requirements
  • Ransomware survivability testing
📋

Incident Response Readiness

Documented IR plan existence and currency. Tabletop exercise history. Roles and responsibilities. Breach notification obligations mapped to applicable regulations.

  • IR plan documentation and currency
  • Tabletop exercise history
  • Notification obligations (state, HIPAA, etc.)
  • Retainer relationships (IR firm, legal)
🧑‍💼

Vendor & Third-Party Risk

Material vendor inventory, security questionnaire status, contractual security obligations, and supply chain risk exposure relative to your insurance coverage scope.

  • Material vendor inventory
  • Vendor security questionnaire status
  • Contractual security obligations
  • Supply chain exposure mapping
📄

Application Accuracy & Attestation

Line-by-line review of your current or upcoming cyber insurance application against documented evidence of controls — so what you attest to is what you can actually prove.

  • Application review against evidence
  • Gap identification before submission
  • Attestation language review
  • Pre-submission remediation priorities
Ideal Engagement

When to Bring In the CIPU Assessment.

→

Approaching Renewal

60–90 days before your cyber policy renews. Enough time to identify gaps, remediate the highest-priority items, and submit an accurate application with supporting evidence.

→

First-Time Applicants

Organizations obtaining cyber coverage for the first time. Understand what underwriters will ask, what controls you need to demonstrate, and what your options are before you apply.

→

Post-Incident or Audit Finding

Following a security event, a carrier audit, or an internal finding that raises questions about your coverage posture. Establish your current baseline and a documented path forward.

→

M&A or PE Transaction

Acquirers and investors increasingly require cyber coverage confirmation as part of diligence. CIPU establishes the target's posture and documents what remediation is required pre-close.

The Deliverable

What You Get at the End.

A scored, documented assessment report — not a slide deck. Built to put in front of your broker, your board, or your acquirer.

📊

Scored Assessment Report

Domain-by-domain scoring across the six control areas. Current state documented against insurer criteria. Gaps flagged with severity classification — what's a coverage risk versus what's a best-practice gap.

🗺️

Prioritized Remediation Roadmap

Ranked remediation actions by coverage impact and implementation complexity. What to fix before your renewal, what can wait, and rough effort estimates for each item.

Application Review & Evidence Summary

Readout with your broker or advisor to walk through findings and support the application or renewal conversation. Available as an add-on to the standard engagement.
